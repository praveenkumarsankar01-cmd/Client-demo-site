Root Canal Treatment

Pain that lingers after something hot or cold, or wakes you at night, usually means the nerve inside the tooth is inflamed. A bite test and a digital X-ray show which tooth it is, and whether a filling is enough or it needs a root canal.

Questions

Before your first visit

Answers to what patients most often ask us. For anything else, send us a message.

Ask on WhatsApp
Will treatment hurt?

Most treatment is done under local anaesthetic, and we apply numbing gel before any injection. You can raise a hand to pause at any time.

I'm nervous about dentists. Can you help?

Yes. Tell us when you book and we will give you a longer first appointment, explain each step before we do it, and stop whenever you ask. Conscious sedation is also available.

How will I know what my treatment costs?

Each treatment page shows its starting price. After your examination you receive an itemised written estimate, and nothing starts until you have agreed to it.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. EMI options are available for larger treatments such as implants, braces and clear aligners. Reception can give you the monthly breakdown.

Can I get advice before I travel to Chennai?

Yes. Send your X-rays or photos on WhatsApp and we will outline your options and likely costs, so you can plan your visit. See international patients.

Is there parking near the clinic?

Street parking is available in front of the clinic.
