Questions

Before your consultation

Answers to what patients most often ask us. For anything else, call or send us a quick enquiry.

How much is a consultation?

A consultation with Mr Roy costs €150. He examines you, explains your options and gives you an accurate price for your treatment.

Who will carry out my treatment?

Mr Roy personally performs every treatment and procedure at Excellence Medical, from a single anti-wrinkle area to minor surgery.

Does it hurt?

Most treatments use very fine needles, with numbing cream or local anaesthetic where needed. Procedures are done under local anaesthetic, so you stay awake and comfortable.

Is there any downtime?

Most non-surgical treatments need little or none. Silhouette Soft and minor surgery may mean a day or two of mild swelling, and you go home the same day.

Will the result look natural?

That is the aim of every treatment. Mr Roy uses conservative doses and works with your natural features, and reviews you afterwards to fine-tune if needed.

Do I need a referral for minor surgery?

No. You can book directly, there is no HSE waiting list, and same-day treatment is also available.
