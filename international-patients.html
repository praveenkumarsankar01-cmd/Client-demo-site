How it works

Most of the planning happens before you travel, so your time in Chennai is spent on treatment, not waiting.

  1. Share your records

    Send recent X-rays, photos of your teeth and a note about what you would like changed, on WhatsApp or email.

  2. Video consultation

    The specialist talks you through the options and sends a written treatment plan, timeline and estimate in rupees, with the US dollar equivalent.

  3. Treatment in Chennai

    Appointments are booked back to back, and our in-house laboratory keeps waiting time between visits to a minimum.

  4. Aftercare at home

    You leave with your records and X-rays in English, and we stay in touch on WhatsApp for any questions.

Questions

Travelling for treatment

If your question isn't here, message us — we usually reply within a few hours.

Can you give me a price before I travel?

Yes. From recent X-rays and photos we can give a written estimate. We confirm it after examining you in person, and if anything changes we agree the new cost before continuing.

How do I pay?

By international credit or debit card, UPI, or bank transfer. Payments are taken stage by stage as treatment is done, not all in advance.

What if I have a problem after I fly home?

Message us first — many questions can be answered from a photo. You leave with full records so a local dentist can help if needed, and any warranty repair on our work is done free on your next visit.

Which treatments suit a single trip?

Root canals, crowns, bridges, veneers, whitening and dentures can usually be completed in one trip. Implants need two trips because the bone needs three to four months to heal.
